# Value Proposition Canvases

Each canvas is a design hypothesis. The customer profile appears on the left and the proposed call-center value map on the right.

## Alarmed cardholder

| Customer profile | Value map |
|---|---|
| **Jobs:** stop possible misuse, identify the charge, file the correct claim and regain control | **Services:** agent copilot, secure authentication, transaction search, card-control tool, evidence checklist and case tracker |
| **Pains:** wait time during active risk, unfamiliar descriptors, repeated authentication and uncertainty about money | **Pain relievers:** prioritize risk questions, rank transactions, retrieve authorization facts and prevent duplicate actions |
| **Gains:** fast protection, plain-language explanation and proactive updates | **Gain creators:** action receipt, distinction between temporary and final decisions and scheduled status updates |

**Fit:** the caller can protect the instrument and create an auditable case in one guided interaction while rules and people retain liability authority.

## Uncertain recognizer

| Customer profile | Value map |
|---|---|
| **Jobs:** identify the charge and choose the correct path | **Services:** merchant enrichment, candidate ranking, confirmation workflow and clarification prompts |
| **Pains:** opaque descriptors, too many transactions and pressure to call the event fraud | **Pain relievers:** use amount, currency, date and merchant similarity; ask discriminating questions; abstain at low confidence |
| **Gains:** a short candidate list, time to verify and a safe stop | **Gain creators:** fewer unnecessary claims and a confirmed transaction link when the case proceeds |

**Fit:** the exact transaction is confirmed before the system recommends a Visa dispute family.

## Persistent resolver

| Customer profile | Value map |
|---|---|
| **Jobs:** correct an improper charge, prove prior effort and reach closure | **Services:** interaction history, evidence intake, case timeline, deadline engine and appeal path |
| **Pains:** repeated storytelling, missing evidence, unclear owner and missed promises | **Pain relievers:** summarize prior contact, prevent duplicate cases, check evidence completeness and flag stalled work |
| **Gains:** one continuous record, visible gaps, reliable update time and understandable outcome | **Gain creators:** context follows the case and every channel sees the same status and owner |

**Fit:** the caller continues the same case without reconstructing it and can see what the issuer needs or will do next.

## Assisted caller

| Customer profile | Value map |
|---|---|
| **Jobs:** authenticate and complete intake with guidance while preserving dignity | **Services:** language preference, accessibility workflow, editable transcription, teach-back and alternate verification |
| **Pains:** jargon, rushed questions, inaccessible links and fear of making a costly mistake | **Pain relievers:** simplify questions, expose uncertainty, support corrections and keep accent out of eligibility decisions |
| **Gains:** pace control, clear steps, accessible follow-up and a human available | **Gain creators:** the same service entitlement regardless of language, disability, age or channel preference |

**Fit:** human guidance stays primary and AI reduces clerical burden without becoming a barrier or hidden decision-maker.
